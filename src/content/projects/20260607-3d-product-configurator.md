## Revolutionizing E-commerce

Traditional product photography requires shooting every possible combination of a product. For a sneaker with 50 color options and 10 materials, that's 500 unique images per style. Our 3D configurator eliminates this overhead entirely.

## Technology Stack

### WebGL & Three.js
We built the entire rendering engine using WebGL and Three.js for maximum compatibility across devices:

- **No plugins required** - Works directly in modern browsers
- **Cross-platform** - Desktop, tablet, and mobile support
- **Performance optimized** - 60fps rendering on mid-range devices

### PBR Materials
Our materials system uses Physically Based Rendering to achieve photorealistic results:

- Accurate light interaction simulation
- Real-time material blending
- Environment mapping for reflections
